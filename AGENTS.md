## Core scientific correction

The old temporal 80/10/10 evaluation confounded split membership with object
position. In the car recordings, the official test frames were the final valid
frames, where the moving car was near the edge of the observed trajectory.
Consequently, dynamic-car AP30 `os1=0.09` did not measure general os1 quality;
it measured generalization to that final trajectory segment.

The original presentation video made this easier to misinterpret:

- It showed all 372 raw frames from experiment 1, including train, val, test,
  and invalid frames.
- Its timeline divided raw frame count into 80/10/10, although the real split
  was created after invalid frames were removed.
- Good os1 predictions on train/val positions therefore appeared next to a
  claim based only on the held-out final positions.

Do not restore the claim that os1 generally cannot detect the moving car. The
defensible statement is that os1 showed a position-dependent generalization
failure, while fusion was robust on that held-out segment.

The completed experiment-held-out cross-validation supersedes even that result
as the primary project conclusion. Pooled dynamic-car AP30 is
`merged=0.877`, `os0=0.885`, `os1=0.831`; fold-mean AP30 is respectively
`0.869`, `0.896`, `0.839`. All three views detect the moving car well on
complete held-out recordings, and os0 is slightly best. The old
`os1=0.09 -> merged=0.90` headline was a temporal-split artifact and must not
be used as the final result.

Fusion still has the highest aggregate mAP in every fold and the best pooled
person AP/localization and bicycle localization. The defensible fusion claim
is therefore best balanced overall performance, not necessity for car
detection.

## Label-based dynamic/static evaluation variant (2026-07-12)

The official dynamic/static sub-evaluation ignores a prediction only when
it matches a non-target GT at the evaluation threshold. Sloppy but
attributable boxes on static-labeled objects therefore counted as false
positives of the dynamic ranking; this depressed merged dynamic-car AP60
(0.739) and person AP60 — see the decomposition section in
`results/CROSS_VALIDATION_RESULTS.md`. Because the manual labels
distinguish static from dynamic, a named variant now uses them:
`exp_eval_crossval.py --ignore-floor 0.25` excludes predictions
overlapping non-target GT with IoU >= 0.25 from target-subset rankings.
Outputs: `results/crossval/CROSS_VALIDATION_STATIC_AWARE.{json,md}` on the
server, copied to `results/` locally. All-object metrics are bit-identical
to the official ones. Key shift: dynamic mAP merged 0.777 -> 0.836,
os0 0.700 -> 0.710, os1 0.734 -> 0.756; merged then leads AP60 in every
class. The presentation's dynamic-object slides use this variant with a
footnote; the official metric (default arguments) remains unchanged and is
the comparability reference. The CenterPoint tables were recomputed under
the same variant (`--results-root results/crossval_cp_eval`, outputs
`CENTERPOINT_CV_STATIC_AWARE.{json,md}`): CenterPoint's AP60 deficit vs
PointPillars then spans all three classes (clearest for bicycles); merged
remains CenterPoint's best view.

## Frozen cross-validation protocol

The experiment-held-out protocol was frozen on 2026-07-11 before inspecting
any cross-validation test result. Full details are in
`results/CROSS_VALIDATION.md`.

Three paired folds are used:

| Fold | Complete test experiments |
|---|---|
| 1 | 1 car, 4 bicycle, 7 person |
| 2 | 2 car, 5 bicycle, 8 person |
| 3 | 3 car, 6 bicycle, 9 person |

Important details:

- The intersection of valid timestamps across `merged`, `os0`, and `os1` is
  used: 2,122 physical frames. Thus every view receives identical timestamps.
- View-specific annotations remain unchanged.
- Complete held-out experiments are test data and are never used for model or
  checkpoint selection.
- Within the other six experiments, the final 10% are validation data.
- Ten preceding frames per training experiment are excluded as a temporal
  guard between train and validation.
- Fold sizes are:
  - fold 1: train 1,239, val 146, test 677, guard 60
  - fold 2: train 1,172, val 137, test 753, guard 60
  - fold 3: train 1,227, val 143, test 692, guard 60
- PointPillars is initialized from the KITTI three-class checkpoint and trained
  for 50 epochs with the existing GT-Sampling policy.
- Seed is fixed at 42.
- Every fold/view has its own train-only GT database. Leakage assertions and
  dataset-loading smoke tests passed for all nine combinations.

Do not change folds, guard length, common-frame policy, GT-Sampling settings,
seed, or checkpoint selection while these runs are active. Any later ablation
must be named and reported separately.

is the inference artifact and has no new untouched test score.

## Bicycle false positives

The presentation's error-pattern slide now uses a generalized OOF ghost-box
diagnostic across all three classes, stored in `results/GHOST_BOXES.json` and
computed by `mmdetection3d/tools/analysis_tools/exp_ghost_boxes_crossval.py`.
A ghost box in this chart is a prediction with score >= 0.3 that cannot be
greedily matched to an unused GT box of the same class at 3D IoU >= 0.3. On
the 2,122 common test frames the totals are `merged=984`, `os0=1520`, and
`os1=949`; class counts (person/bicycle/car) are respectively
`510/315/159`, `948/157/415`, and `329/372/248`. These are general false
positive boxes, so they also include duplicate and poorly localized boxes;
not every counted box is an empty spatial hallucination.

The bicycle-only analysis below remains the detailed spatial/temporal
forensics behind the memorization examples, but the main presentation chart
must not describe ghost boxes as a bicycle-only failure mode.

The visible one-frame "ghost bicycles" are real model false positives, not a
viewer bug. Existing analysis found 42 merged predictions at score >= 0.3 with
no bicycle GT within 2 m. They cluster at a few positions and have three main
causes:

1. real but unlabeled/misclassified static structures,
2. sparse reflection/under-floor clusters,
3. scene-position memorization near bicycle routes seen during training.

Some frames also contain bicycle/person hypotheses inside a higher-scored car
because NMS is class-specific. Evidence and exact frame forensics are in
`results/DATA_AUDIT.md`, section 11.

Already tested conclusions:

- GT-Sampling is not the primary cause; baseline has nearly the same FPs.
- A z < -2 m point filter did not reduce ghosts and hurt dynamic-car AP. Do not
  adopt it as the standard pipeline.
- Cross-class NMS removes contained duplicate-class boxes and is metrically
  neutral; it is acceptable as output cleanup.
- A debug display threshold of 0.3 intentionally exposes low-score hypotheses.
  Practical inference should use validation-selected per-class thresholds and
  preferably temporal track confirmation/background support checks.
